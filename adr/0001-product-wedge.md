# ADR-0001: LOA-to-Delivery-Challan is the first product wedge

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-06

## Context

The full legacy specification contains many valuable post-award capabilities, but building all of them before customer use delays learning and revenue.

## Decision

The first sellable workflow is LOA upload and review, Work creation, Delivery Challan issue/cancel/PDF, quantity ledger, signed-copy storage, and audit history.

## Consequences

Measurement Books, GST integrations, procurement, broad reporting, and advanced permissions are deferred. Architecture work that does not directly enable the wedge requires explicit justification.
